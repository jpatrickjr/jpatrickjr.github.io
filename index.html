Local Cosmos

Infrastructure for thriving local economies.

An activation company for local economies. Local Cosmos helps communities understand what's actually happening in their place — the businesses, events, destinations, and people — and keep that picture current, so more of it can be discovered, supported, and put to work.

The Problem

Local is fragmented.

Local information is distributed across tourism websites, business directories, event calendars, spreadsheets, social networks, GIS systems, registration platforms, internal databases, and institutional knowledge.

When something changes, organizations frequently discover it manually — if they discover it at all.

  • Tourism websites
  • Business directories
  • Event calendars
  • Spreadsheets
  • Social networks
  • GIS systems
  • Registration platforms
  • Internal databases
  • People's heads

A business closesCaught manually

Hours changeCaught manually

Ownership changesCaught manually

An event movesCaught manually

A new destination appearsCaught manually

The challenge isn't simply storing local information.

The challenge is knowing when reality changed.

The Thesis

Local information should behave like infrastructure.

Roads, water, and power are maintained continuously because a place depends on them. Communities need the same for what they know about themselves — a continuously improving understanding of their businesses, places, events, organizations, culture, activity, and relationships.

Local Cosmos is being designed around that idea.

Meet LOIS

LOIS

Local Operating Information System

In active development

LOIS is the intelligence layer behind Local Cosmos. It's being designed to help communities detect changes, verify information, maintain trusted local records, understand relationships, and activate that information across local experiences.

Visit meetlois.com ↗

  1. 01

    Detect

    Notice when something in a community changes — a new business, new hours, an event that moved.

  2. 02

    Verify

    Confirm what's true with sources and local people, and keep track of where each fact came from.

  3. 03

    Organize

    Maintain trusted, structured records of businesses, places, events, and organizations.

  4. 04

    Connect

    Understand relationships — who organizes what, where it happens, and who's involved.

  5. 05

    Activate

    Put current information to work across discovery, media, and local experiences.

The model LOIS is being built around. Capabilities are in development and being tested in the field.

The Ecosystem

One system, several ways in.

Local Cosmos is the umbrella. LOIS is the shared intelligence underneath. On top sit the experiences where that information meets people:

FestiveFly — a local discovery experience centered on events, places, businesses, and community activity.

Pull Up — a curated local discovery and media layer helping people find things worth doing in their communities.

Trukfoo — a platform focused on food trucks, mobile food, and local food discovery.
